Primordial

You’re a single bacterium that just slipped into a human host. Your goal is to infect and capture its organs: the gut, liver, lungs and brain. Swim with your mouse, your finger, or WASD.

Gold sugar gives you ATP. Purple amino acids are the raw material for proteins. Build proteins to unlock enzymes: they let you digest new food and survive new dangers.

Immune cells hunt you: neutrophils chase, T cells are fast, B cells shoot antibodies, and macrophages are huge. Red blood cells are food once you have protease. Sugar becomes glucose, and you choose how to turn it into ATP: fermentation, anaerobic or aerobic respiration. Press Space to divide: daughter cells follow you, forage, speed up infection, and take over if you die. Evolve to reach deeper organs.
